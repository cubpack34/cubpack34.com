Once again we are collaborating with Troop 33 and the Fanwood Presbyterian Church to collect food to support our local community.

We will be distributing bags in town **October 17** and collecting bags **October 24th by 11am**.

Scouts will be provided bags and labels to distribute. Labels will need to be attached to the bags.

Scouts should wear their uniforms during these activities and must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

We will have more details as we get closer to the event.
