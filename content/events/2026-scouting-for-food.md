## Details

**Bag Drop Off**: October 24th before noon<br />
**Cleanup Crew**: October 24th noon - 3pm<br />

## How to Participate

{{% steps %}}

### Sign-up

* Add your information next to routes that you want to take on the sign-up sheet.
* We also need families for "Cleanup Crew" that can drive around and look for late/missed bags.

### Bag Pick Up

* Once your bags and labels are ready, you will receive an email with pickup instructions.

### Bag Distribution

* Staple a label to the bag.
* Place the bag folded neatly in the mailbox, tie the bag to front door handle or hand directly to the resident if they are outside.
* Scouts need to be wearing their class A/B uniform when distributing bags.

### Bag Collection

* Walk the route checking for bags out on porches.
* Check carefully as sometimes the bags are obscured from view.
* Collect the bags you find along your route.
* Scouts need to be wearing their class A/B uniform when collecting bags.

### Bag Drop Off

* Bring collected bags to Fanwood Presbyterian Church between 9am and 12pm.
* Enter the parking lot from McDermott Pl. following the cones.
* Troop 33 will be on site to assist.

### Cleanup Crew

* Drive the streets checking for bags left behind.
* Collect whatever you find and bring them to Fanwood Presbyterian Church.
