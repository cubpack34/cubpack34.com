## Big Turkey Hunt Story

Scouts will each craft their den's character in preparation for the hunt. Once everyone is ready, our Cubmaster will begin to read the story of the Big Turkey Hunt. Whenever scouts hear their animal's name in the story, they should raise up their crafts and make the animal's sound.

| Den    | Character | Sound             |
| :---   | :---      | :---              |

| Tiger  | Bee       | Buzz, buzz!       |

| Bear   | Bear      | Growl, growl!     |

## Sock Drive

<img src="/images/bhs-logo.webp" style="max-width: 400px; height: auto;">

This year we are collecting socks to donate to Brandon's Heart and Sole. Their mission is to take socks off the most wanted list at homeless shelters and for people in need. Socks are one of the least donated items while being one of the most needed. Since 2021, they have donated over 50,000 pairs and worked with over 30 shelters and organizations. You can learn more about Brandon's Heart and Sole <a href="https://www.brandonsheartandsole.org/">here</a>.

As a fun twist, the donated socks will be used for a sock ball fight at the end of the meeting. 

If you are able, please bring new, in package socks to the meeting to donate.
