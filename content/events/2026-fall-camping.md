## Who can Attend

Family camping is at the heart of what we do! Our campouts are open to all registered scouts and their siblings, and we require a parent or guardian to stay on-site for the duration of the event to join in on the fun.

## Food

* **Meals Provided:** The Pack will be grilling hamburgers and hot dogs for lunch and dinner, as well as providing bagels and coffee in the morning!

* **Stay Hydrated:** Please bring a reusable water bottle for each family member. We’ll have refill stations set up at the campout to help reduce plastic waste.

* **Side Dish & Snack Sign-Up:** We’d love help rounding out our meals! If you’re able to bring sides, snacks, or drinks to share, please sign up below.
