## Race Check in

* Each car must pass inspection by the official inspection committee before it will be allowed to compete.
* If your car does not pass inspection, a pit stop area is available to make any necessary adjustments.
* The Inspection Committee has the responsibility to disqualify those cars that do not meet derby rules.
* Once your car passes inspection, only the race committee members can touch it.
* If your car loses a wheel or is otherwise damaged during the race, you will be granted 5 minutes to make necessary repairs.

## Race Structure

* 8 Divisions (Each Den, Pack Final, Sibling)
* Pack Final Division includes the top 3 from each den.
* Each car will race in 3 heats, once in each lane. The best time will be used.
* Top 3 in each division will receive a trophy including for pack final and sibling brackets.
* Trophies will be awarded immediately after each division.
* All racers will receive a derby patch including siblings.

## Main Car Rules

* All 4 wheels must touch when the car moves. No raised or tilted wheels are allowed.
* Car must weigh at least 4 oz and no more than 5 oz.
* Max length is 7 inches long.
* Max width 2 3/4 wide including wheels.
* Must clear track and undercarriage height of 3/8" or more.
* The front end must be at least 1/2 inch wide. *The starting gate has a pin that the cars rest on; if the front is too narrow the car will slip and not start properly.  Split * front ends will not work either.*
* No precut/premade car kits or after-market wheels (color and/or heavily modified).
* Graphite must applied prior to the day of racing, to keep the track and race area clear.
