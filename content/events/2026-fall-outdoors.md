## Who can Attend

Our outdoor event is open your scouts, their families and their friends.

## Activities

* Field Games

* Loop Playground

* Hiking

* Trailside Nature & Science Center

* Food

* S'mores over Charcoal Fire

## Food

* **Meals Provided:** The Pack will be grilling hamburgers and hot dogs for lunch and dinner!

* **Stay Hydrated:** Please bring a reusable water bottle for each family member. We’ll have refill stations set up to help reduce plastic waste.

* **Side Dish & Snack Sign-Up:** We’d love help rounding out our meals! If you’re able to bring sides, snacks, or drinks to share, please sign up below.
