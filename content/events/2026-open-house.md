## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can join Cub Scouts?
Cub Scouting is open to both boys and girls in Kindergarten through 5th Grade (ages 5 to 10).

### Do parents need to stay for the meeting?
Yes! Cub Scouting is a family-centered program. Parents or guardians should attend meetings alongside their children to share in the fun.

### Are we obligated to join if we attend?
Not at all. This open house is completely free with zero pressure. Come let your child build and launch a rocket, meet the leaders, and see if Pack 34 is a good fit for your family.

### What if we can't make this date?
All the information we plan to cover at this meeting is available on this website in the "Upcoming Events" and "About" sections. If you have any questions, please email us at cubpack34@gmail.com
