Save the date for a fun-filled weekend at Winnebago Scout Reservation!<br />

Camp one night, both nights or just come for the day on Saturday.<br />

Whether you stay for the whole weekend or just visit for the day, there’s an adventure waiting for every Cub Scout.<br />

Registration is open now until Friday, May 15, 2026 at 11:59pm. This event is limited to the first 600 people that register. If you plan to go, please do not wait until the last minute to register.

## Details

**When**: Friday, May 29 – Sunday, May 31, 2026<br />
**Where**: Winnebago Scout Reservation<br />
**Cost**: $30 for Scouts, siblings, and adults (Children under 5 are
FREE!)<br />
**Food Cost**: $10 per person (zelle to cubpack34@gmail.com). The food budget covers breakfast, lunch, dinner and campfire snakes on Saturday and breakfast on Sunday.
