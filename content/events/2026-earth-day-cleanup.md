**Registration is required to participate in this event**

## Overview

Join us for this year's Earth Day Celebration Community Cleanup.<br />
Let's work together to cleanup our community.<br />
We will have garbage bags available.<br />
All scouts have a service requirement as part of one of their required adventures.<br />
Participation in this cleanup is a great opportunity to satisfy this requirement.<br />
<br />
**Please make sure to bring your own gloves.**

## How to Register

1. Navigate to community pass with link above or click <a href="https://register.capturepoint.com/scotchplains">here</a>.
2. Browse Activities for **Scotch Plains Recreation**.
3. Select **2026 - Events**.
4. When you get to Programs, select **2026 Spring: Earth Day in Scotch Plains (Community Clean-Up)**.
5. When filling out the program form
    - Select that you are participating in a group and provide **Cub Scout Pack 34** as group name.
    - Select **Crestwood Wildlife Sanctuary** as your preferred location.
    - Make sure to select a shirt size for each participant.
