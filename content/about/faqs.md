## General

### Where do we meet?

We meet in the Multipurpose Room at Evergreen Elementary.

### What schools do we serve?

We serve Evergreen, Brunner and School One.

### What ages can join?

Cub scouts is open to all scouts Kindergarten through 5th grade.

### Can my daughter join the pack?

Yes. We are a family pack and open to both boys and girls. The one caveat is that any den with a girl member is required to have a female adult volunteer registered to the pack.

### How can I reach you with additional questions?

You can email us at cubpack34@gmail.com

### Who charters our pack?

Our pack is chartered by American Legion Post 209.

### What BSA Council are we in?

We are part of the Pioneer District of the Patriots' Path Council.

### Do parents need to be present? Can I drop off my child?

Parents need to remain present at all cub scout meetings.

## Pack Structure and Leadership

### Pack vs. Dens?

### Who leads the pack? Who leads each den?

The pack is lead by a committee of people including the Pack Committee Chair (PCC), Cubmaster, and Chartered Organization Rep (COR). Each den has a den leader and at least one assistant den leader. All leaders are parents of scouts in the pack.

### Are leaders trained and background checked?

All adult volunteers are background checked during registration with the BSA. Volunteers are also required to participate in Youth Protection Training and training specific to their roles.

## Meetings and Events

### How often do the pack and dens meet?

Scouts will typically have 1 pack and 1 den meeting per month.

### How long are the meetings?

Den meetings are usually 1 hour and pack meetings are usually 2 hours.

## Ranks and Achievements
### How does my scout earn each rank?

Ranks are earned by completing all adventure requirements for that rank. Adventures can only be completed for a rank while your scout is a part of that den. You can read more about the required adventures for each den [here](https://www.scouting.org/programs/cub-scouts/adventures/).

### What if I missed an adventure with my den?

While we prefer to complete adventures together as a den, all adventure requirements can be completed at home with your child. 

## Pack Dues

### How much are the pack dues?

$80

### What do pack dues cover?

* Registration and Training of Adult Leaders
* Advancements/Awards/Trophies/Patches
* Meeting Supplies
* Pack Events (Pinewood Derby, Blue and Gold Celebration, etc)

### How do I pay the pack dues?

Pack dues should be sent through Zelle to cubpack34@gmail.com. Please include "Pack Dues" and your scout's name in the memo. If you are unable to use zelle, please reach out and we can make other arrangements.

## Uniform

### What is the class A uniform

The class A uniform is the standard attire worn by Cub Scouts during pack meetings and official events. It promotes equality, fosters a sense of belonging and provides a place to display earned achievements. This uniform consists of the navy blue dress shirt with appropriate patches, neckerchief, slide and hat.

### What patches are required on class A uniform

On the left sleeve, the council patch should be on top along the shoulder seam with our pack number (34) just below

On the right sleeve, the american flag should be on top (the flag patch should be presewn on all shirts and does not need to be purchased seperately).

Above the left pocket, place the world crest patch surrounded by the world crest ring.

### What is the class B uniform

The class B uniform is our pack t-shirt. This shirt is perfect for outdoor activities, campouts, hikes, etc. 

### Where can I get the uniform

The cub scout class A uniform can be purchase from the [Scout Shop](https://www.ppcscoutshop.org/).

Pack t-shirts can be purchased from the pack for $15. Please reach out if you need one. Your scout's first pack t-shirt will be provided free when joining the pack.

### What parts of the uniform do I need?

Check out the [Uniform](/about/uniform/) page for details.

### When does my scout need to wear their uniform

The class A uniform is required when participating in ceremonies or attending events representing the cub scouts. We will note in event details when the class A uniform is required.
