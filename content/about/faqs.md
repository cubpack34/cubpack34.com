## General

### Where do we meet?

We meet in the Multipurpose Room at Evergreen Elementary.

### What schools do we serve?

We serve Evergreen, Brunner and School One.

### What ages can join?

Cub scouts is open to all scouts Kindergarten through 5th grade.

### Can my daughter join the pack?

Yes. We are a family pack and open to both boys and girls. The one caveat is that any den with a girl member is required to have a female adult volunteer registerd to the pack.

### How can I reach you with additional questions?

You can email us at cubpack34@gmail.com

### Who charters our pack?

Our pack is chartered by American Legion Post 209.

### What BSA Council are we in?

We are part of the Pioneer District of the Patriots' Path Council.

### Do parents need to be present? Can I drop off my child?

Parents need to remain present at all cub scout meetings.
