## Recommended Items

1. Tent
2. Ground Cloth (Tarp for under tent)
3. Sleeping Bag
4. Reuseable Water Bottle
5. Backpack or Duffle Bag
6. Jacket or Sweatshirt
7. Hat
8. Class A Uniform (for opening ceremony)
9. Pack 34 Activity Shirt (Class B Shirt)
10. Sturdy Shoes or Boots
11. Extra footwear & socks (in case you get wet)
12. Rain Gear (Poncho or Rain Jacket)
13. Clothing for each day plus an extra set
14. Flashlight
15. Whistle
16. Pajamas
17. Personal Care Kit (soap, shampoo, deodorant, toothbrush, toothpaste, hair brush / comb)
18. Bug Repellant
19. Sunscreen
20. Towel or Wash Cloth
21. Folding Camp Chairs

## Other Items

1. Fishing Gear
2. Camera
3. Notebook and Pen or Pencil
4. Musical Instrument (Campfire Songs)
5. Watch
6. Compass
7. Sunglasses
8. Sleeping Mat or Air Mattress

## Restricted Items

1. Guns (Toy or otherwise)
2. Any Alcoholic Beverages (You will be asked to leave if you do)

{{< callout type="error" >}}
Please do not keep food or other smellable items (soap, shampoo, etc) in your tent at any time. All food items should be stored in the bear car. Smellables should be in air tight containers. **We take these precautions because we do not want any unwanted visitors in our campsite. (Raccoons, Bears, anything that has four legs and teeth!)**
