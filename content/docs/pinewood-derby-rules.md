## Administrative Section
*The following rules incorporate the Stock Class race rules from the Greater New York Council (GNYC) World
Championship Pinewood Derby.*

1. **NEW CAR**: Car must be built this year. Cars from earlier years are not eligible.
2. **OFFICIAL KIT**: The car MUST be built by the Scout (parents may assist) from the “Official Pinewood Derby
Kit” supplied by an authorized Scout store. No other car kits or parts from other car kits are acceptable.
3. **SELF STARTING**: Cars will be started simultaneously by gate lever. No part of the car will extend past the starting
line when positioned on the track. Any car with a pointed nose which does not rest against the starting pin properly
may, at the discretion of the race officials, lead to disqualification. The car may be run backwards if necessary to
avoid disqualification.
4. **LUBRICATION**: Only dry powder graphite or white BSA Wheel and Axle Lubricant may be used to lubricate
wheels and axles. No oil, grease or silicone lubricants areallowed.
5. **LOOSE ITEMS**: Any decoration or added weight must be permanently attached to the car with glue, nails or
screws.
6. **RACE OFFICIALS**: To ensure fairness, Race Officials will make the final judgments on the qualifications of all
cars. Race Officials may suggest reasonable adjustments to cars to avoid disqualification
7. **IMPOUND AREA**: After registration, cars will be quarantined; no cars may leave the impound area. Re-
lubricating of wheels will not be permitted once the cars arequarantined.
8. **CRASHES, TIES and DISPUTES**: If a car leaves the track or a wheel comes off before the car reaches the finish
line (and the car can be repaired), the race will be rerun. A one-time, three-minute pit stop may be allowed to repair the
car. The owner will make all repairs in the presence of a race official and have the car re-inspected. All disputes are
subject to the resolution of the Race Officials.
9. **RACE DIVISIONS**: Races will be held for each of the following divisions: Lion, Tiger, Wolf, Bear, Webelos, and
Arrow of Light. Each car will race in 3 different heats (number of heats is equal to the number of lanes on the track) in
a different lane each time to eliminate track bias.
10. **SCOUT ENTRANTS**: All Scouts cars must have been built during the 2024-2025 scouting year.

## Basic Car Specifications
*In Conformance with the World Championship Pinewood Derby Stock Class*

### Dimensions and Weights
1. Cars must have been built during the 2024-2025 Pinewood Derby racing season (September 2024 through January 2025)
using an Official BSA Pinewood Derby Kit or official BSA components purchased through an authorized BSA retailer such as
a Scout shop. Non-official BSA parts sold by an official BSA retailer are prohibited. Aftermarket car kits, non BSA precut
kits, or cars purchased whole or in part from another racer, company, internet entity, or other are prohibited. Cars must be
constructed by the racer and an adult, Scouts honor.
2. Width, including wheels, axles, and any fenders or cosmetics, must not exceed 23⁄4 inches(6.99cm).
3. Length shall not exceed 7 inches (17.78cm). If fenders are present, they cannot extend beyond the front or rear car body.
4. The car must not exceed the clearance height of the finish gate or no more than 5 inches in height.
5. Weight shall not exceed 5.0 ounces (141.75 grams) measured on a scale accurate to 1/10th of an ounce. Overweight cars must
be reduced to running weight or below before they can be entered. The official race scales shall be considered final.
6. Cars must clear the center rail of the track, which is 1 5/8 inches wide by 1⁄4 inch tall along the entire length of the car.
Minimum clearance between the entire bottom of the car and the bottom of the wheels shall be 3/8 of an inch.
7. No part of the car may protrude beyond the track start gate starting pin. The front edge of the car must not be more than 1 inch
above the track surface and be at least 1/2 inch wide at the center of the car.
8. Any details added must be within length, width, and weightlimits.
9. No part of the car or any attachment to the car may be capable of coming into contact with the track other than the wheels.

### Examples of Prohibited Items
1. Any type of magnets, springs, washers, bearings, bushings, nylon shims, axle guards, wheel weights, wheel well weights,
inner wheel air dams (also known as air deflectors or air shields), inner or outer wheel covers – clear or otherwise, or any
item internal or external to a factory BSA wheel, moving weights, liquid lubricants actively wet or dried, or suspension
systems of anytype.
2. Any item not included in an official BSA Pinewood Derby Car Kit or not purchased through an authorized BSA
retailer, except cosmetic items or fenders. Aftermarket axles are notpermitted.
3. Starting devices, finish line devices, propellants, or propulsion systems.
4. Wet paint, sticky substances, glass, excessively fragile parts, electronic or lighting devices (if lights are on the car they
must be turned off).
5. Bearings or solid one-piece rod style axles or aftermarket purchasedaxles.
6. Axles and wheels attached to any device that mechanically alters rotation orspin.
7. Loose objects on or in the car. All weight must be securely fastened or embedded in or on the car.

### Wheels and Axles
1. Only official BSA wheels from an authorized BSA retailer can be used; colored BSA wheels are permitted. Note, wheels
with the letters BSA and China or any other indication other than a standard BSA wheel from a BSA authorized retailer is not
permitted.
2. All lettering and numbering, both inside and outside, must remain complete and unaltered from factory die-casting, and be
completely visible with no obstruction of any kind. No wheel covers of any type or any name, inside or outside, may beused.
3. The fluting and other BSA markings on the outside wheel area must remain visible and unaltered.
4. Outer wheel surface (tread area only) may be LIGHTLY sanded, shaved, or polished to remove minor surface imperfections,
mold casting burrs, and correct off center wheel bores; but total wheel diameter may not be reduced below 1.170 inches
(29.72mm). Outer wheel surface must not be reshaped or have the contour changed in any way in an attempt to minimize
tread contact or alter aerodynamics. Wheels with ANY flex to the tread or sidewall when squeezed or twisted in inspection
will not pass. Lathing outer wheel tread is allowed within the confines of the above specs.
5. Tread surface must be flat and parallel to the wheel bore and have a thickness of 0.036 inches consistently across the tread.
6. Wheels may not be reversed (hub facing away from the car body) and must be mounted in standard position.
7. Coning the hubs and truing the inside tread edge is allowed, as long as overall wheel width is not reduced below 0.36 inches
(9.14 mm).
8. NO MATERIAL may be removed from the inside wheel tread, sidewall, hubsurfaces.
9. Minimum outside diameter of wheel must be equal to or greater than 1.170 inch (29.72mm), and must maintain the ridges on
the outer edge.
10. Inspectors reserve the right to measure the wheel with calipers to verify dimensional compliance with official wheel size
restrictions. If graphite is not present or oil is suspected, graphite will be applied to all wheels.
11. The weight of the wheel shall not be increased or decreased. No material such as glue, fingernail polish, or tape, may be
added to the inside of the wheel increasing itsweight.
12. Wheel Bore treatment is allowed including polishing and/or tapping. Wheel bores may not be filled and re-drilled to alter bore
diameter or to achieve better fit with the axle (no material may be added to the wheels).
13. The factory axle slots in the block MUST be used and visible. The wheel base must be 4 3/8 inches apart from axle to axle. The
slots may be trued or straightened.
14. Axles may be canted for alignment purposes.
15. Axles may not be bent.
16. Axles may be glued into the car as long as the inspectors can determine solid axles have not been used. The tip of
the nail axle must be visible to allow inspectors to verify nail axles are being used
17. Axles may be polished and any burrs or mold excess may be sanded or filed off.
18. If axles are suspect, the racer will be asked to pull them for inspection and that racer will be responsible to re-
install them.
19. Please note: There are after-market modified wheels available that have been LIGHTENED. This is usually done
by turning the wheels on a lathe and removing material from the inside of the wheel. These wheels are NOT
allowed and can be EASILY RECOGNIZED at inspection. Cars with third partymanufactured or
modified wheels will not be permitted to race.
20. No "3 wheeled" cars are allowed. All 4 wheels must be in contact with the track at all times. The wheel
tread does not have to be flat on the track.
Wheels determined by the inspectors to be out of compliance must be replaced to compete. Official BSA wheels will be
available.
